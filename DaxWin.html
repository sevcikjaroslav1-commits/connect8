How DaxWin trades

Mechanical + Fundamental + Correlation — MT4 Expert Advisor

DaxWin is the product of 20 years of active participation in financial markets — through multiple bull and bear cycles, liquidity crises, and geopolitical shocks. That accumulated experience is encoded into a ruleset the EA executes without hesitation or fatigue.

The system operates on a purely mechanical principle — every entry, every exit, every position size is determined by predefined rules with no room for emotion or second-guessing. But the rules themselves were forged from deep market understanding: they account for fundamental catalysts such as central bank decisions, macro data releases, and geopolitical events.

An additional layer of cross-market correlation analysis filters signals before execution. Positions on US30 and GER40 are evaluated in the context of each other and of broader risk sentiment — ensuring the system does not act in isolation, but reads the full picture the way an experienced trader would.

20 years of market knowledge

Mechanical execution
Entries, exits, and position sizing are fully rule-based. The EA executes without emotion, override, or manual interference — discipline is built into the code.
Fundamental awareness
The system's logic incorporates scheduled macro events — rate decisions, employment data, inflation prints — adjusting exposure around high-impact windows.
Cross-market correlation
Signals are validated against correlated instruments and broader risk sentiment. The system trades with the market's grain, not against it.
