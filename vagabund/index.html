2. Stručne

Appka nezbiera, neukladá na server ani nezdieľa tvoje osobné údaje. Nemá registráciu, reklamy ani analytické nástroje. Všetko, čo appka zachytí, sa spracúva len v tvojom mobile.

3. Povolenia a na čo slúžia

  • Poloha (presná) – na výpočet vzdialenosti a smeru k dronom. Android ju zároveň vyžaduje na skenovanie Bluetooth a Wi-Fi. Tvoja poloha sa používa len v mobile a nikam sa neodosiela.
  • Zariadenia v okolí (Bluetooth a Wi-Fi) – na príjem vysielania Remote ID z dronov a na rozpoznanie Wi-Fi sietí dronov.
  • Wi-Fi a internet – na skenovanie Wi-Fi a na načítanie podkladovej mapy.
  • Vibrácie – na upozornenie, keď je dron blízko (ak si to zapneš).

4. Údaje o dronoch a pilotoch

Drony podľa predpisov EÚ o bezpilotných lietadlách verejne vysielajú identifikáciu a polohu (Remote ID), často aj polohu pilota. Appka tieto verejné vysielania prijíma a zobrazuje len na displeji tvojho mobilu. Neukladá ich trvalo – dron zmizne z appky približne 1–2 minúty po tom, čo prestane vysielať – a nikam ich neodosiela. Sériové a registračné čísla dronov appka nezobrazuje.

5. Nastavenia

Tvoje nastavenia (napr. zapnuté zdroje, zvuk, vibrácie) sa ukladajú len v pamäti mobilu. Pri odinštalovaní appky sa vymažú.

6. Mapa a služby tretích strán

Na zobrazenie mapy appka načítava mapové dlaždice zo služby OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) a mapovú knižnicu Leaflet zo služby unpkg.com. Pri tom sa týmto serverom, ako pri bežnom prehliadaní webu, odošle tvoja IP adresa a technické údaje o prehliadači. Nevieme ich ovplyvniť; platia pre ne zásady ich prevádzkovateľov (OpenStreetMap Foundation). Žiadne údaje o dronoch, pilotoch ani tvoja poloha sa im neodosielajú.

7. Prepojenie s Connect8

2. Summary

The app does not collect, store on a server or share your personal data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. Everything the app receives is processed only on your device.

3. Permissions

  • Precise location – to calculate the distance and direction to drones; Android also requires it for Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning. Your location is used only on the device and is never sent anywhere.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi) – to receive drone Remote ID broadcasts and recognise drone Wi-Fi networks.
  • Wi-Fi and internet – for Wi-Fi scanning and to load the base map.
  • Vibration – to alert you when a drone is close (if enabled).

4. Drone and pilot data

Under EU drone regulations, drones publicly broadcast their identification and position (Remote ID), often including the pilot's position. The app receives these public broadcasts and shows them only on your screen. It does not store them permanently – a drone disappears from the app about 1–2 minutes after it stops broadcasting – and never sends them anywhere. Drone serial and registration numbers are not displayed.

5. Settings

Your settings are stored only on the device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

6. Map and third-party services

To display the map, the app loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) and the Leaflet map library from unpkg.com. As with normal web browsing, your IP address and technical browser data are sent to these servers and are subject to their operators' policies (OpenStreetMap Foundation). No drone data, pilot data or your location is sent to them.

7. Connect8

The button in Connect8 only opens the app. The app does not exchange any data with Connect8.

8. Your rights

As the app keeps no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. You can contact us at the e-mail above and you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (dataprotection.gov.sk).
